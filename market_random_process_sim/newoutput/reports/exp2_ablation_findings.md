# Experiment 2 Ablation Findings

- Most negative mean beta: `Both constraints` at wealth multiplier `0.5`.
- Lowest log-deviation variance: `Both constraints` at wealth multiplier `0.5`.
- Interpretation: cash and inventory constraints should be read as mechanisms that alter feasible order flow. The ablation table identifies whether boundedness is mostly generated by buyer-side cash scarcity, seller-side inventory scarcity, or their interaction.
